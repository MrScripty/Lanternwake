# Lanternwake — full story bible

## Dramatic contract

Lanternwake is a literary coastal mystery about the difference between receiving information and surrendering judgment. The player inhabits Ada Vale, an adult archivist arriving at her late uncle's lighthouse. A pressure-driven instrument writes tomorrow's events in her own handwriting. Solving its mechanism intersects with correcting the record of six adult workers who died in a historical flood.

The fantasy is not omnipotence. It is being useful in a strange room: listening well, naming what a record actually supports, making a plan with other people, and discovering that a future can matter without ordering the present around.

The ending must satisfy the mechanical mystery, the historical investigation, and the emotional relationship with the deceased keeper independently. A scientific explanation does not absolve wrongdoing. Correcting a public lie does not resurrect anyone. Loving a person's useful gifts does not require endorsing their concealed harm. The machine can be lost while the truth and the community survive.

### Tone

Salt air, domestic detail, restrained dread, dry humor, practiced kindness, and a storm that is dangerous because water and machinery are physical. No jump scares. No monster reveal. No hidden cult. No supernatural afterlife messages. No dream-ending invalidation. No twist in the documented cause of Ivo's death. The mystery becomes more human as its physics becomes stranger.

People are allowed to laugh beside grief. They do not constantly deliver polished thematic maxims: practical tasks, awkward silences, competing priorities, and small errors should remain audible. The authored manuscript contains deliberate repetitions of procedures to build trust, but editorial review should remove redundant moral explanation before adding more runtime.

### Player agency

The authored action spine is fixed. Ada arrives, investigates, preserves evidence, identifies her authorship, sends two traces, participates in a safe release, and leaves with a plan to return on her own terms. Free input can ask questions, express skepticism, change tone, request comfort or precision, acknowledge hurt, or decline optional conversation. It cannot create new physical actions or contradict witnessed facts. Ada remains an authored character with agency; the player controls how conversation is approached, not the universe.

There is one ending with room for varied local relational emphasis. Do not describe the game as branching romance or an unrestricted sandbox. A rejected suggestion is not a moral failure. The next required clue never depends on flattering a character, guessing a hidden phrase, or using a model.

## Cast

### Ada Vale — 34, estate archivist, viewpoint

Ada works with neglected collections and questions of provenance. Her skill is distinguishing observation from interpretation; her vulnerability is wanting a clear description to remove emotional ambiguity. She learned early conservation principles from Ivo and resents needing his approval after becoming independently competent. She postponed visiting him while expecting that a better, more reciprocal conversation would eventually become possible.

**Want:** finish the inventory competently and leave before the house assigns her a life. **Need:** preserve complexity without turning it into an endless obligation. **False solution:** one final revelation will tell her whether Ivo was good or bad, and therefore how she must feel. **Arc:** the catalogue becomes accurate enough to contain both affection and wrongdoing; Ada can return voluntarily rather than inherit his silence.

She is brown-skinned with short dark wavy hair, a plum layer under a charcoal raincoat, practical boots borrowed from Nessa, and a moss-colored satchel. Her professional posture is forward-leaning attention; emotional strain appears in over-careful object placement rather than dramatic trembling. She is not a blank player avatar and does not need a trauma confession to justify caring.

Her family remains on the mainland and alive. The childhood book annotation, mother's song, sister's call, and ordinary work messages establish a life beyond Saltmere. Their purpose is to keep leaving meaningful, not to introduce another mystery. Names and details not authored should remain unspecified.

### Nessa Ward — 47, harbor master

Nessa was nineteen in 1998 and helped distribute warm photocopies of the obsolete evacuation plan. She did not design the plan or command the failed rescue. Her remembered participation is emotionally consequential without being equivalent culpability. As harbor master she built named-route and repeat-back protocols because implicit assumptions had proven dangerous.

**Want:** keep today's people safe and today's work clear. **Need:** distribute responsibility rather than privately hold every edge. **False solution:** if she personally checks enough things, nothing can fall between people again. **Arc:** she acknowledges leaving Tomas isolated with a shared question, delegates a real checklist to her deputy, and makes the current operation accountable to others' checks.

Her kindness is logistical: bread, boots, a working key, a quiet garden, a useful route, a sentence that does not make the user's grief perform for her. She does not become the voice of absolute authority. Her own procedures are reviewed afterward. Her orange harbor coat has navy repaired cuffs; short iron-gray hair, freckles, heavy-weather stance. Warmth appears when she makes room, not when she becomes uncharacteristically verbose.

### Tomas Rook — 43, sound conservator

Tomas's father Ewan was one of the six adult West Quay crew. Tomas left the island, trained in audio conservation, and returned for what became a years-long attempt to recover the original response record. He dislikes the idea that grief makes him an unreliable researcher, while remaining alert to how badly he wants a particular answer.

**Want:** replace an accusation against the crew with a source-grounded public account. **Need:** let evidence matter without requiring it to restore every lost relationship or consume every future day. **False solution:** one more document must be recovered before ordinary life can resume. **Arc:** he receives the truthful record, speaks publicly within his actual authority, preserves the crew as people, and recommits to restoring a choir recording for a living person.

He has olive skin, curly dark hair graying at the temples, a teal sweater, brown utility vest, and mismatched headphone pads. He is warm, occasionally wry, and never a comic relief sidekick. Exactness is care, not emotional anesthesia. He can be angry without becoming cruel to Ada. He is not required to forgive Ivo or sound generous immediately.

### Dr Sera Wynn — 39, coastal-systems hydrologist

Sera arrived to inspect aged pressure equipment, not expecting a revolution in physics. She wants the phenomenon to be real and admits that desire. In earlier river-warning work she learned that an improved forecast is not a complete benefit if intended recipients cannot receive or act on it.

**Want:** understand a unique apparatus responsibly. **Need:** let curiosity and professional opportunity remain subordinate to other people's safety. **False solution:** enough technical qualification will resolve every ethical question. **Arc:** she distinguishes measurements from decisions, preserves bounded evidence, and accepts safe destruction of the active state without secretly trying to restart it.

Dark skin, close-cropped black hair, mustard raincoat, bottle-green workwear, field notebook and caliper. She draws connections to prove to herself that she has looked. Her humor concerns practical limits; she never delivers an omniscient metaphysical lecture. At the end, she still has unanswered questions. Scientific restraint is not disbelief in what she actually observed.

### Ivo Vale — 72 at death, recorded presence

Ivo died six weeks before Ada's arrival from a medically documented cardiac arrhythmia while making tea. His death is not suspicious, staged, caused by the chronograph, or secretly preventable by arbitrary time travel.

He was competent, exact, funny in narrow domestic ways, useful to neighbors, and capable of serious self-protective failure. In 1998 he accepted supervised operation despite unresolved channel-routing problems. When the rescue instruction failed, he prioritized repairing his system instead of using the direct line in the remaining interval. He later helped conceal the corrected map and allowed a false crew-disobedience account to stand. His private complete testimony survived because he kept it, but keeping it privately was not restitution.

His letter points Ada toward people and records; it does not solve the mystery. Do not transform it into a master plan proving he knew every event. He was not the hidden author of Ada's traces. Do not give him a final ghostly approval or make his domestic kindness a redemption coupon. Only authored recordings speak in his voice. Portrait in a rust cardigan; never a living walk-on in present-day scenes.

## Saltmere

A fictional inhabited temperate island linked to the mainland by ferry. The old tower was completed in 1906; its postcard often says 1905, which Ivo corrected. Modern navigation equipment remains separately maintained. Shutting down the old pressure installation does not remove the coast's only navigation light.

The island depends on deliveries, specialized workers, shared storage, the hall, and mainland services. Its independence is an affectionately punctured myth. The built landscape includes a public harbor, west maintenance quay, obsolete east approach, sheltered corrected west passage, inland high road, locked lower cliff path, keeper house, archive in the former wireless station, and a high community hall.

The school building no longer has an active classroom; the current island roll during this event consists of adults. It hosts classes, meals, repairs, and emergency shelter. Do not imply children are missing during evacuation. The hall is not a sixth full 3D set in the current build; the five-location stage vocabulary frames its logistics through dialogue, radios, and transitions.

## Physical mystery: exact rules

1. A natural layered resonant inclusion in basalt was named "phase glass" by surveyors. The term is historical shorthand, not a claim about ordinary silica glass.
2. Engineers routed a pressure loop around it and coupled the loop to a manual input stylus and receiving stylus in the tower.
3. In the maintained differential configuration, input movement appears at the receiver exactly **23 hours and 17 minutes earlier**. The story depicts one self-consistent loop.
4. It carries continuous stylus motion: traces and handwriting. It does not transmit bodies, thoughts, memories, spirits, recorded voices, or arbitrary objects.
5. There is no supported arbitrary date selector, no access to 1998 from today, no parallel timeline, no rewritten photograph, no death reset, and no observed contradiction test. Characters explicitly acknowledge the limits of their experiments.
6. The clock interval is not the cause of a moral obligation. A trace can be interpreted wrongly, just as a correct measurement can become a bad instruction.
7. The historical tapes are ordinary magnetic recordings. Their provenance remains assessable even if someone doubts the extraordinary physics.
8. The bell counterweight's controlled descent into an empty cradle mechanically raises the bypass gate. No person enters the loaded shaft or tide chamber during release.
9. Equalization fractures the already stressed resonant inclusion, ending the observed receiving effect. The damaged path remains isolated afterward. No secret sequel restart.
10. Blank receiver paper means no recorded trace. It is not a symbol with evidentiary force and does not predict Ada's death.

- Day 2, 17:52: Sera inputs the harmless three-stroke/circle pattern corresponding to the Day1 18:35 record. She acknowledges this is a known-pattern consistency test, not a blind demonstration of unrestricted choice.
- Day 2, 18:58: the team explicitly calculates the two remaining input times.
- Day 2, 19:15: Ada inputs the cup message, using knowledge of a completed harmless event and its useful viewing angle.

## Historical chronology and responsibility

In 1998, the experimental installation's advance pressure reports were promoted toward public flood-warning use before a channel-routing issue was resolved. Ivo signed for supervised use, believing he could personally manage the weakness. The corrected working chart existed; the issued emergency map omitted the sheltered west approach.

At21:04, Channel A reports WEST LEVEL HOLDING, a hydrographic measurement, not an order to a boat. At21:06, coordinator Pell instructs rescue craft to use the obsolete east route. At21:08, Ivo sends the west correction on A, expecting temporary routing to relay it to the instruction channel. At21:09, he recognizes there is no valid repeat-back. Instead of immediately using the direct harbor line, he continues trying to restore the system. The relevant window closes; six adult workboat crew die. The story does not assert a guaranteed alternative survival outcome, only a documented lost opportunity and failed response.

Later, the published summary recasts the measurement as a disobeyed vessel hold. Ivo explicitly directs omission of the corrected map and admits in complete deposition that he protected his position. The public narrative becomes a false account of crew disobedience. Administrative circulation, editing, and institutional retention failures also matter. The recovered sources warrant independent review; the protagonists do not pronounce every legal liability over a tape deck.

## Chapter causality

### I — The Inventory

Arrival establishes a functioning community, a finite job, and a life Ada could return to. Household inventory teaches provenance through things that are not all clues. The tower produces the two traces. Tomas offers procedural skepticism and an ordinary recording. Ewan's loss and the incomplete archive become human stakes. End: Ada has questions, no mechanism, and a frightening blank strip she must not overinterpret.

### II — The Wrong Channel

The archive separates measurement from instruction. Copy provenance reveals how apparently clean text can conceal overlapping, degraded sound. Nessa's working chart and a tabletop route reconstruction expose the missing sheltered approach without claiming an alternate guaranteed rescue. Ivo's explicit omission note changes suspicion to supported concealment. The cup event confirms an advance-description match and directs attention to calibration hardware. End: the extraordinary and historical mysteries now share physical infrastructure, but neither substitutes for evidence about the other.

### III — What the Water Kept

Safe survey reveals stylus-only pressure coupling and a physical gravity bypass. Sera admits desire for the phenomenon while bounding her claims. Original tapes remain ordinary. Ivo's signoff establishes a known unresolved weakness. Nessa and Tomas address years of isolation; all three living islanders retain ordinary obligations. The controlled consistency test establishes the fixed interval and Ada's distinctive mechanical trace. End: complete testimony is available, the reservoir is a current safety issue, and no one can pretend the blank future is an instruction.

### IV — The Night Ledger

Full testimony names Ivo's choices and the coordinator's wrong route. Six ordinary working lives accompany the operational record. The team preserves verified copies independently of the tower, defines who can speak for whom, and publishes a bounded source-based statement. Evacuation logistics make "no one" a verifiable list, not a rhetorical comfort. Ada identifies herself as the trace author. End: she chooses a safe plan and an honest catalogue without calling either forgiveness.

### V — An Open Horizon

Ada sends both traces at their consistent input times. The second is grounded in complete readiness checks. Controlled bell descent opens the bypass; everyone is safe; receiving ends. Return-to-shelter accounting completes the operation. Morning brings repairs, acknowledged evidence, independent review, ordinary music, family contact, and a catalogue that permits corrections. Ada leaves and plans to return voluntarily. Last image: paper with room for what happens next, not a final oracle.

## Ending acceptance criteria

The player can answer who wrote both messages, what physically transmits them, why the interval matters, why 1998 cannot be rewritten, how the cup clue works, what the bell actually does, why Ivo is responsible without being the only responsible person, how the crew were misrepresented, where the evidence survives, and why blank paper is not a death sentence. Every answer must be available without LLM output. The final emotional image belongs to Ada and the living community, not to Ivo granting approval.
