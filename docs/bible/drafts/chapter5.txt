## ch5_s3 | The Bell Falls | lantern_room | night | ada,nessa,tomas,sera
narrator: Sera watches the manometer until the rising column touches the marked release range. She reads the number aloud. Nessa repeats it. Tomas repeats the time. You check the exit one final time and keep your hands away from controls that are not yours.
sera: Release condition reached. All measured conditions remain within the tested procedure.
nessa: Releasing brake in three. Two. One.
narrator: The lever travels through a short stiff arc. In the shaft, the great counterweight begins to descend. The bell does not leap from the tower or tear through the floor. It moves under the brake, slowly enough for you to see the dark rim pass each painted mark. Its bronze side touches the safety guide with a low note that deepens as it falls.
narrator: Below you, the linkage pulls the bypass gate upward. Water enters the cleared outflow channel. The pressure line's thin ringing breaks into a broad rush, then softens. Sera reads the differential aloud as it falls. Nessa keeps the brake within its marked range. Tomas does not step forward for a better photograph.

## ch5_s4 | A Morning Without Instructions | harbor | morning | ada,nessa,tomas,sera
narrator: Morning does not arrive cleanly. It leaks through the storm's trailing cloud onto puddles, loosened shutters, and a harbor full of ropes that need drying. Nobody has drowned. Several people slept badly. The hall kettle tripped a fuse twice. A storage shed lost half its roof. The island has a list of repairs, and for once the list can remain a list.
narrator: You wake in a borrowed camp bed with your inventory bag under your feet. Across the hall, Tomas sleeps with a folded coat over his eyes. Nessa is already at a table, arguing mildly with someone about whether a damaged bench counts as operational infrastructure. Sera sits beside her with two cups of tea and the expression of a woman trying not to write a paper before breakfast.
ada: Did the pressure stay down?
sera: Yes. Independent readings agree. The bypass is secured open, the input mechanism isolated, and the lower path closed pending inspection. No receiving traces after equalization.
ada: You sound disappointed and relieved.
sera: Both are evidence of my having understood the situation.
narrator: The ferry will resume in the afternoon if the harbor inspection clears. You do not have to decide immediately whether to take it. The freedom feels unfamiliar after an evening of clocks.
nessa: The records service acknowledged the supplementary files this morning. They want the originals deposited for conservation assessment when transport is safe. Tomas is arranging it with the families informed.
ada: And the council?
nessa: Emergency meeting next week. Independent review requested. The public summary is flagged as disputed pending correction. Nobody gets a neat ending by lunchtime.
tomas: I am fiercely in favor of a messy, documented middle.
narrator: His voice comes from beneath the coat. He removes it and blinks at the light. There is no transformation in his face, no visible version of grief after proof. But when Nessa places the source acknowledgment beside his tea, he reads it without bracing himself first.

## ch5_s5 | A Catalogue With Room | keeper_house | afternoon | ada,nessa,tomas,sera
narrator: The keeper's house is dry except for a narrow line beneath the south window. You put a towel there and add the seal to the repair list. The room has not become less complicated overnight. It has become possible to work in it without expecting the next drawer to decide your life.
narrator: You finish the blue cup's entry. Ceramic pencil cup, repaired before arrival, broken during documented pressure-line vibration. Fragments retained for potential conservation. Associated calibration discovery recorded separately. You resist the sentence that would call it the object that changed everything. Objects deserve descriptions that do not ask them to be heroes.
ada: Do you want the handle for the exhibit?
tomas: Only if it belongs in the account. Not because it's photogenic.
sera: It belongs in the calibration provenance. Also, it is extremely photogenic.
nessa: Put both in the notes. Different headings.
narrator: The chronograph entry takes longer. You describe the brass housing, the receiver, the input platen, the documented interval, the preserved traces, and the shutdown conditions. You separate the observed facts from the unresolved physical explanation. You write that no evidence supports arbitrary travel to other dates, changed pasts, transported people, or a message after the final shutdown.
ada: And the blank strip?
tomas: Unmarked receiving paper. Retained.
ada: That may be my favorite line in the whole catalogue.
narrator: In the estate inventory, you include the testimony without euphemism. Ivo's papers document his role in an unsafe handover, failed correction, and misleading public record. Other sources establish the wider institutional failures. You do not make his preserved confession into restitution.
